## Baseline and scope split

1. **`packages/thread-address-core`**: Already ported in full to `crates/omo/thread-address-core` at upstream dev commit `0eaecf33d7` (`crates/omo/thread-address-core/parity.d/1.md`), which sits at the tip of `packages/thread-address-core` in `v5.1.83` (`git diff 0eaecf33d7 v5.1.83 -- packages/thread-address-core` is empty). Every symbol and test added across `v5.1.80..v5.1.83` (`id_shaped`, `device_offline`, `ResolveDeviceOptions::exact_id`, `here` without a device id, `device_reasons`) is already present and tested in `crates/omo/thread-address-core/tests/address_suite.rs`.
2. **`packages/omo-senpi/src/components/thread/address`**: Introduced in #14305. Its pure and local layers (`types.ts`, `refusals.ts`, `device.ts`, `ladder.ts`, `bare.ts`, `resolve.ts`, `index.ts`) are ported to `crates/omo/components/maho-omo-gateway/src/thread/address/`. Its cross-device account/probe layers (`account.ts`, `holder.ts`, `on-device.ts`, `probe.ts`, and `testing/two-devices.ts`) are ported too, over the `RemoteThreadSurface` ports of `thread/remote/ports.rs` (`RemoteClient`, `RemoteSessionsPort`, `AliasRegistry`); `resolve_address` is async and takes the surface as `ResolveContext.remote` (absent: upstream's no-account behaviour). A live device-service implementation of those ports is not part of this track.
