## Scope

The range does two things to this layer. It removes thread bindings (#14312: the relay engine, its store ops and the
six binding tools) while keeping the binding-era tables in the schema, unwritten, for an omo still on schema 14
(#14382); and it adds what a sender needs to learn the fate of a send: a retained delivery receipt reports an outcome
(#14623), `thread_read { delivery_id }` reads it, and one drain pass starts one turn that carries every queued
delivery with its lane decided at admission (steer-all). Everything that needs the account's other devices
(`RelaySessionHost`, the live device-service listing adapter) or the consumer's intake write is `blocked` or `gap`
for the reasons the v5.1.83 ledgers and thread-remote's v5.1.84 ledger already record; the store reads those legs
would call are ported and tested.

## Open items

- **Tool-receipt wrapper.** `thread/tools.ts` `execute` (thread-core's path; thread-core's ledger hands it to this track) calls `toolReceiptBegin`/`Settle`/`Release` around every tool but `thread_send`/`thread_handoff`, replays a stored result as `deduplicated` through `currentResult`, answers `idempotency_conflict` and `idempotency_in_progress`, and hands `thread_create` its `retrying`. mhc's `tools.rs` records no receipt and `create_tool.rs` passes no `retrying`, so `tool_receipt_*` have no production caller yet. Porting it means a per-facade registry of receipts that could not be settled, `currentResult` for receipts an older omo build stored in the shared store, and an `idempotency_key` on the control tools' schemas; it changes the answer of every non-send tool, so it is left for its own change.
- **Consumer.** `createDeliveryConsumer` (intake write, `delivery_outcome_notice`, `get_delivery_outcome`, the notices `reportDrained` sends) and a client behind `DeliveryServicePort`; see the `dde26d7b4c` row.
- **Fork capability.** `open_session_fork` (`ThreadHost.capabilities`, `SessionHost.forks`, `forkRefusal`); see the `aef88019f8` row.
- **`SQLITE_IOERR` recovery.** The second trigger of `7f068c4d32`: an I/O error reruns the call once on a fresh connection upstream; here the call runs once.
