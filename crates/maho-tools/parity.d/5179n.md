The record is senpi's version-1 JSON (`startedAtMs`, `bootId`, `sessionId`, `hostPid`, `hostStartedAtMs`), so a
record written by an omo or senpi engine and one written here resolve through the same reader. The bash,
terminal and codemode kernel spawn sites belong to the senpi core tracks; `attest_session_spawn` is the call
they make after a spawn and `SessionSpawnAttestation::release` the call after the child exits.
