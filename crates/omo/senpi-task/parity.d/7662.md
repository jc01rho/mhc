`run_task_output` keeps its three-argument form (no cache, as upstream with `statusReads` omitted);
`run_task_output_with_status_reads` is the four-argument form. The cache is a bounded
(1024) oldest-first deque keyed by (caller session, task id); the fingerprint hashes
`final_response`, `error_message` and `run_stats` with SHA-256 like upstream.

The new upstream case in `renderers.test.ts` (no-progress row) is ported in
`src/tools/output/renderers_tests.rs`. The component side (tool cache in `TaskToolsDeps`,
accepted `session_compact` forgetting tool and RPC reads, RPC `no_progress` bounding) is in
`maho-omo-task` `src/tools.rs`, `src/component.rs`, `src/task_rpc_bridge.rs`,
`src/task_rpc_codec.rs`, with tests in `tests/component_recovery.rs`, `tests/task_rpc_bridge.rs`
and `tests/task_rpc_snapshot.rs`.
