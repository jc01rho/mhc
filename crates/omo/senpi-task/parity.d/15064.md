## Notes

- `HostSessionClient` is scoped to one open: an owner is created per `open_with_policy` call, so a
  generation moves only through `detach()`. The production caller of a mid-open detach is
  `HostAssembly::schedule_recovery`: a reattach attempt registers its owner in the recovery round's
  `InFlightOpens`, and a round whose bound ran out detaches them before the child is declared lost.
- `open_with_policy` for a fresh start passes no registry, so nothing outside the open call holds that
  owner. A teardown that should end a fresh open needs a registry of its own; none exists today.
